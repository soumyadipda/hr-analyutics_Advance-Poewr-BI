#                                                   -  HR Analytics Dashboard  -

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition across departments, job roles, age groups, cities, and promotion status — built to help HR leadership move from reactive to data-driven retention decisions.

![HR Analytics Dashboard](https://github.com/soumyadipda/hr-analyutics_Advance-Poewr-BI/blob/main/HR%20dashboard.png)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Software Used

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Power BI Desktop** | Dashboard design & data modeling |
| **Power Query (M)** | Data cleaning & transformation |
| **DAX** | Calculated measures (Attrition Rate, Avg Salary, Avg Experience, etc.) |
| **Excel / CSV** | Source dataset |

---

## 📌 Business Insights

- **4,298** total employees tracked, with **2,008 exits** — an overall **attrition rate of 46.7%**
- **35–44 age group** shows the highest attrition (538 exits), signaling risk in the mid-career workforce
- **Legal, Admin, and HR** departments report the highest exit counts (250+ each)
- Nearly **half of all exits (49.7%) happened despite a recent promotion** — pay and role changes alone aren't retaining talent
- **Chennai and Pune** lead city-wise attrition, useful for location-specific retention planning
- Attrition **peaked in 2023 (239 exits)** and has since trended downward to 178 in 2026

---

## 📈 Performance

- Reduced manual reporting time from hours to **real-time, self-service insights**
- Enabled **segment-level drill-down** (department → role → city) for targeted action
- Surfaced the promotion-attrition gap, informing revised retention policy discussions

## ❗ Problem

The organization was losing nearly **1 in 2 employees**, but this trend was buried across spreadsheets with no unified view. HR leadership lacked visibility into *which* departments, roles, age groups, and locations were driving attrition — making retention strategy reactive instead of preventive.

---

## ✅ Solution

Built a single-page, interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates attrition data across **department, job role, age group, promotion status, city, and year** into one real-time view. This enables HR teams to instantly spot high-risk segments and act on retention **before** losses happen, instead of analyzing exits after the fact.
